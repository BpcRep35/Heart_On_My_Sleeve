-Heart On My Sleeve-

-Project-

A 501c3 Organization



Mission Statement And Values:

Our business will build a bridge between different religious groups with a fun way to display their contributions in a form a sustainably sourced cotton clothing. Also a lifetime membership for world changers, we are a fun, friendly, fashion forward foundation that is dedicated to changing the world one hoodie at a time. Once we build together it will move beyond to many non profit organizations, not just different religious organizations.





How Our Organization Works:

Our organization is a 501c3 Non-Profit organization. Our goal is to break down biases within each organization we work with by selling our clothing at a reduced price by ensuring the organizations know that our company encourages resolving the differences of these organizations by thinking about the bigger picture. This is to ensure that we help others and spreading love to our communities. This promise to help our communities will ensure more connections with the people who are in these churches, while helping them give back and clothing those who need it. Our organization will have our logo on it, but we also encourage each church and organization we do business with to design their own logo and we will put it on the clothing. This ensures that your church or organization can sell them to raise funds, and also have a unified impact on the communities they serve.



Lifetime Membership:

For our world changers, we offer a lifetime membership by making patches for their organizations and being able to buy other organizations patches. This supports the other organizations, as well as shows the unified front of our organization to change the world.
